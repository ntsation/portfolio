## About

I'm Nathan Marinho de Souza, a Machine Learning Engineer from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, with a
degree in Computer Science from UNESA (2021–2025). My path started back in college, when I
joined BNDES (Brazil's national development bank) as a Data Engineering intern. I spent two
years building and optimizing distributed pipelines with Apache Spark, automating workflows in
Python, and helping structure the bank's corporate Data Lake, on top of putting together Power
BI dashboards and React interfaces to give business teams visibility into strategic data. That
was where I learned, hands-on, that data without reliable infrastructure behind it never turns
into a decision.

From data engineering I gradually moved into machine learning. I joined A3Data as a Machine
Learning Engineering intern, modeling business problems with scikit-learn and wiring those
models into scalable APIs with FastAPI, while automating data and ML pipelines and helping
adopt Infrastructure as Code practices to reproduce cloud development environments. That stage
gave me the missing link between training a model and actually shipping it to production
reliably.

I was promoted to Machine Learning Engineer at A3Data, where my scope grew considerably. I
built FastAPI services running on AWS EKS and SageMaker to predict shipping prices and delivery
times for a pharmacy chain, reliably handling around 23,000 requests a day. I also led the
architecture of a multi-agent system for educational support using LangGraph and LangChain,
orchestrating more than ten specialized agents across flows like the "Teacher Workflow" and the
"Student Workflow." Alongside that, I shipped end-to-end ML pipelines to production with
Kubernetes, Docker, and MLflow — with versioning, traceability, and monitoring through
OpenSearch — provisioned cloud infrastructure with Terraform across AWS, GCP, and Azure, and
built event-driven architectures with SQS and SNS for asynchronous processing.

Today I'm a Machine Learning Engineer at Globo, where I manage cloud infrastructure on GCP and
orchestrate large-scale data ingestion pipelines for the company's social media presence,
audience metrics, and anti-piracy efforts, always with solid DataOps practices. I also
maintain and optimize production Machine Learning pipelines for topic modeling and relevance
analysis that support Globo's digital content distribution intelligence, and keep it all
running with CI/CD and Docker containerization, from development through monitoring.

Day to day, I move between classic machine learning — scikit-learn, feature engineering,
mathematical optimization — and Generative AI: LLM integration, RAG, LangChain, LangGraph,
CrewAI, and prompt engineering, always backed by MLOps (MLflow, Kubeflow), data engineering
(SQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Spark), and solid software engineering practices like SOLID, design
patterns, automated testing, Git, and CI/CD. What drives me most is taking a complex
requirement and turning it into an architecture that's resilient, scalable, and has a
measurable impact on the operation.

I'm currently investing actively in English, focused on technical fluency to work in global
environments and international projects.
